# References {-}

```remark
The {-} heading prints the unnumbered References chapter (with a table of contents
entry); the embed below registers refs.bib with LaTeX and prints the entries at
this position without a second heading (heading=none in the dds settings).
Replace refs.bib with your own export (e.g. from Zotero) or add entries to it -
the citation keys used in your text must match its entries.
```
